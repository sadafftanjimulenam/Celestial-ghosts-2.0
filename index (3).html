We explored. We left something behind.

Every journey to another world ends the same way: the engines stop, the batteries fade, and the crew goes home or the signal goes quiet. The hardware stays.

Rovers, landers and instruments still sit where they stopped, some for more than fifty years. They aren't trash. They are the first chapters of a story you can still read in their dust.

The rover that still waits at Hadley

Apollo 15 brought the first Lunar Roving Vehicle to the Moon. It folded up like a suitcase for the trip, then carried astronauts David Scott and James Irwin far beyond walking distance. When the mission ended, they parked it and left. It is still there.
